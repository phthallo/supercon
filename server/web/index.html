about

Drag to paint. Box and circle draw from corner to corner, and fill makes them solid. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Pan with move, two fingers, or by holding space.

Keys: 1 2 3 colour, b brush, e eraser, l line, r box, o circle, f fill, m move, [ and ] size.

The badge is a 960×672 e-paper screen that only shows white, black and red. It checks for changes every minute, and a redraw takes a while.
